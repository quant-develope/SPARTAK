Pool liner: colour, texture and watertightness in one membrane

The liner decides how your pool looks and how the steps feel underfoot. See colours, textures and the ranges we use.

The liner is the pool you actually see and touch: a PVC membrane that lines the floor, walls and steps as one watertight shell. We use 1.5 mm fibre-reinforced liner with a 10-year written warranty, or 1.8 mm reinforced membranes with a textured, anti-slip surface.
