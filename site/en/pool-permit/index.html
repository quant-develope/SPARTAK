Pool permit in Greece: the small-scale works approval

A pool with under 50 m² of water usually needs a simple approval, not a full building permit. Here are the conditions, in plain English.

The small-scale works approval

In Greece, a private pool with a water surface under 50 m² usually does not need a full building permit. It needs a small-scale works approval (έγκριση εργασιών μικρής κλίμακας): faster, cheaper and without a full set of engineering studies. Before work starts, a licensed engineer signs a declaration covering the structure and the electrics.

The conditions in plain words

ConditionWhat it means
Water surface under 50 m²Only the water counts, not the deck around it
Depth up to 1.5 mPrefab panels are 1.10 or 1.50 m high
Up to 1 m above natural groundAlso applies to raised pools
Excavation or fill up to 1 mThe terrain is not reshaped dramatically
Compact filter unitFilter and pump in a single unit
Planting of the open area keptThe plot's mandatory greenery stays
Engineer's declarationSigned before work starts; we coordinate it

Rules change and depend on each plot (outside town plans, traditional settlements, archaeological zones). The engineer confirms your case before any work. This is general information, not legal advice.

Over 50 m²?

Then a building permit is needed. It is perfectly possible, it just takes longer. If you are a few square metres over, a long narrow pool often solves it.

After the build

A private pool is declared on the E9 property form and affects property tax (ENFIA) and income presumptions. Your accountant will give you the current figures.
