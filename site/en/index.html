Pool types

Three ways to have a pool

Every pool we build is prefabricated: the same steel frame and the same liner. What changes is how the water circulates and how the pool sits on your plot.

Skimmer poolThe simple, popular choice for family homes and holiday houses.Overflow poolWater level with the deck, like a mirror. For villas and rentals.Small or raisedFor town gardens and sloping plots. Less digging, same quality.

Behind the liner

A steel frame and a watertight skin

A prefab pool is not a temporary pool. It is a permanent structure whose walls arrive as factory-made panels and are bolted together on site.

  • 2 mm galvanised steel panels, 1.10 or 1.50 m high.
  • Adjustable braces at every joint keep the walls perfectly vertical.
  • 1.5 mm reinforced PVC liner, welded on site, 10-year written warranty.
  • Flexible, so it does not crack when the ground moves, a real advantage in Greece.

For foreign owners

Building from abroad?

Many of our clients live outside Greece and visit their house a few times a year. We are used to working that way: photos and short videos at every stage on WhatsApp, a written proposal in English, and a pool that is ready and clean when you arrive.

Questions

How long does a prefab pool last?

The panels are galvanised steel made to live in damp ground, and the liner carries a 10-year written warranty. With well-kept water it usually lasts longer, and when it is finally worn, only the liner is replaced.

Do I need a building permit?

For a water surface under 50 m² a small-scale works approval is usually enough. See the conditions.

How much does it cost?

Every pool is priced on its own, depending on size, system, liner, equipment and site access. Ask us and we will send a free written quote.

Do you build on the islands?

Yes, from Mykonos and Rhodes to Corfu and Limnos. See how island projects work.
